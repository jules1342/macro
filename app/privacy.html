Privacy policy

Macro. Last updated 6 September 2026.

Macro is a personal project, made by one person and offered free. It has no accounts, no server run by the developer, and no analytics. It is a set of static files served by GitHub Pages, and everything else happens in your browser.

What is stored, and where

Everything you log (food entries, weights, daily targets, favourites and settings) is written to your own browser storage, on your own device. None of it is transmitted to the developer, and the developer has no way to see it.

Storage is tied to the web address. Clearing your browser data for this site deletes it permanently.

What leaves your device

  • To Anthropic. When you photograph a label or a meal, or describe food in words, that image or text is sent from your browser directly to the Anthropic API so the model can read the nutrition information. This uses an API key that you supply and that is billed to you. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policy and the terms of your account with them.
  • To Google Drive, only if you turn it on. Drive sync is optional and off unless you set it up. When you tap Sync, a single JSON file holding your logged data is written to a folder called "Macro App" in your own Google Drive. The app requests only the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change nothing in your Drive except the files it created itself. Nobody else receives it.
  • Nothing else. No third party receives your data. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Your API key

Your Anthropic API key is stored only in your browser on your device. It is deliberately excluded from every backup: it is not in a JSON export, and it is not in a Drive sync. It is sent only to Anthropic, to authenticate your own requests.

Fonts

The app loads two typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those font files from Google, which means Google sees the request in the ordinary way any website visit is seen. No data you have logged is involved.

Cookies and tracking

None. No cookies are set for tracking, there is no analytics, and there is no advertising.

Deleting your data

  • On the device: Settings, then Reset all data. Clearing site data in your browser has the same effect.
  • In Drive: delete the "Macro App" folder from your Google Drive.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.

Children

Macro is not intended for use by children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions or concerns can be raised at github.com/jules1342/macro/issues.
