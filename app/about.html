Macro

A personal food and macro tracker.

Macro is a small web app for logging what you eat. You photograph a nutrition panel or a meal, or describe it in words, and the app works out calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat, then logs the entry against your daily targets. It also charts weight, calories and protein over time, and estimates maintenance calories from the two together.

It runs entirely in your browser and installs to the home screen. It is built and maintained by one person for their own use, and shared openly.

Open the app: jules1342.github.io/macro
Source code: github.com/jules1342/macro

How your data is handled

  • Everything you log stays in your own browser storage, on your own device.
  • Reading a label or a meal photo uses the Anthropic API, with an API key you supply yourself.
  • Google Drive sync is optional, off by default, and writes only to a folder the app creates in your own Drive.
  • There is no account, no server run by the developer, and no analytics.
